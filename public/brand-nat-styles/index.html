How it works

You need Claude with Claude Code (the Code tab in the Claude desktop app).

1

Get the Style Pack

You already have it. To share it, drag the zip into Claude Code and say "install this skill for me". Claude saves every style, plus this whole guide, on your computer.

2

Open the guide anytime

Say "show me the styles" and Claude opens this page in your browser, even offline. Build your mix and copy the prompt.

Show me the styles
3

Film one take

Vertical, good light, a plain wall above your head. Say each line until it feels right. Claude keeps your last clean take.

4

Order your edit

Paste your mix, then give notes like you would to a real editor.
