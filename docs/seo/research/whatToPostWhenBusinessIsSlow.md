# Research: what to post when business is slow

## Target
- Keyword: "what to post when business is slow"
- Hub: post-ideas
- Source: keyword backlog, post-ideas section, cold start (no Search Console gap fits)
- Reader: an owner of a local trade (home services, shop, clinic) whose bookings or sales have dropped for the season or the week, wondering whether to keep posting and what to say.

## Top 5 Google results (query: "what to post on social media when business is slow" and "social media content ideas during slow season small business") and what they miss

1. **Planoly - Off-Season Social Media Strategies for Seasonal Businesses** (planoly.com/blog/off-season-social-media-strategies-for-seasonal-businesses). Frames slow season as prep time, educate the audience, plan ahead. Misses: no exact post formats, no example captions, written for brands with a content team, not a 1-person shop.
2. **Build a Campfire - Creative Social Content During the Down Season** (buildacampfire.squarespace.com/blog/creative-social-content-during-the-down-season). Suggests batching content and running giveaways. Misses: giveaways need a prize budget most small shops don't have; no script or caption examples; not trade-specific.
3. **SumUp - How to Promote Your Seasonal Business During the Off-Season** (sumup.com/business-guide/seasonal-business/). User-generated content, teaser posts for the next season. Misses: generic advice with no concrete example post, aimed at seasonal retail/travel, not a plumber or salon with a slow week rather than a slow season.
4. **Constant Contact - 38 Social Media Post Ideas for Small Businesses** (constantcontact.com/blog/what-to-post-social-media/). Four content pillars (promotion, education, proof, behind the scenes), general list of post ideas not tied to "slow" at all. Misses: not slow-season specific, no guidance on frequency or why posting through a slow stretch matters.
5. **AlphaGamma / Lilach Bullock pieces**: staff recognition, storytelling campaigns, posting 3-5x/week in winter (unconfirmed number, snippet cut off, not used). Misses: no reasoning tied to local search/visibility, no TikTok or Instagram Reels specific ideas, no caution about going quiet.

Common gap across all 5: none write to a single-location trade owner with a short slow patch (a rainy week, a seasonal dip), none give copy-paste caption examples, none connect posting through a slow period to staying visible for when business picks back up, and none mention TikTok/IG Reels specifically or short-form formats a non-marketer can actually make alone.
