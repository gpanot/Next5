## Top 5 Google results and what they miss

1. **Buffer, "29 Scroll-Stopping Instagram Post Ideas"** (buffer.com/resources/instagram-post-ideas) — 29 categories (share expertise, before/after, tutorials, UGC, giveaways, etc.) with real brand examples. Misses: no posting schedule or calendar, no ready-to-use caption templates, no differentiation for local service businesses (advice applies the same to a clothing brand and a plumber).
2. **Social Media Examiner, "14 Instagram Post Ideas for Business"** (socialmediaexaminer.com/14-instagram-post-ideas-for-business) — 14 categories (carousels, product spotlights, before/after, polls, UGC, contests, trends). Examples are from established consumer brands (@pigletinbed, @shopbando). Misses: no concrete captions a business could copy, no frequency/schedule guidance, no local-service examples (e.g. nothing like "plumbers can post bathroom before/afters").
3. **Hibu, "5 Instagram Post Ideas to Boost Engagement for Small Businesses"** — short, generic list aimed at SMBs; page returned 403 on fetch, not used for claims.
4. **Socialmon, "71 social media post ideas for small business on Instagram"** — long listicle format, not reviewed in depth; same genre as Buffer/SME (long idea dump, no schedule or captions).
5. **Canva templates page / NerdWallet "30 Instagram Business Ideas"** — different intent (templates, and ideas for starting an Instagram-based business), not a true competitor for this query.

**The gap every competitor leaves**: long idea lists with zero execution help. No competitor gives (a) a ready-to-fill caption line next to each idea, (b) a simple weekly schedule a one-person business can actually keep, or (c) idea-to-trade mapping that points an owner straight at the 2-3 ideas that fit their business instead of making them sift through 14-71 generic entries.

## Platform facts (checked today, cross-checked across independent sources since no single page is cited by name in search snippets)

- Instagram carousel (feed) posts: up to **20 photos/videos combined** in one carousel (raised from the old 10-item limit). Each video inside a carousel can be up to 60 seconds; up to 10 videos per carousel. Converging sources checked today: Metricool ("Instagram Carousel Limit Increased to 20 Photos or Videos", metricool.com/instagram-increases-carousel-content-limit), itechguides.com/instagram-photo-limit-you-can-add-up-to-20-items-to-one-carousel, socialrails.com/blog/instagram-carousel-posts-guide. Third-party scheduling tools remain capped at 10 items because of Instagram's API, separate from the native app limit.
