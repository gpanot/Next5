## Every fact, with the source it was read from (all fetched 2026-11-02 with `treg call treg.web.extract`)

NAR 2025 REALTORS Technology Survey (PDF, Sept 2025):
https://cms.nar.realtor/sites/default/files/2025-09/2025-realtors-technology-survey-report-09-18-2025.pdf
- Random sample of 49,233 active REALTORS invited in July 2025; 1,241 usable responses; margin of error 2.78% at 95% confidence.
- eSignature is the most used technology (79%), then social media (75%).
- Asked which technology gave the highest number of quality leads: social media 39%, CRM 23%, local MLS 17%.

NAR 2026 Membership Profile, summary article (more than 5,000 REALTORS surveyed):
https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/even-in-a-tougher-market-realtors-are-holding-their-ground
- Platforms agents use professionally: Facebook 76%, Instagram 57%, LinkedIn 55%, YouTube 31%, TikTok 16%.

NAR 2026 Technology Report news release (22 Sept 2026):
https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/realtors-adopt-technology-to-save-time-and-improve-the-client-experience-nar-report-finds
- Top technologies include AI-generated content tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini and others) at 41%.
- How often agents use AI: daily 23%, weekly 25%, occasional experiments 31%, curious but not tried 9%, not using and no plan to 12%.
- Among agents who use AI: writing listing descriptions 75%, social media posting 56%, emails and follow-up 52%.

NAR 2025 Profile of Home Staging (news release, May 2025; survey Feb 2025, 49,806 invited, 1,266 usable responses):
https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/nar-report-reveals-home-staging-boosts-sale-prices-and-reduces-time-on-market
- Buyers' agents rating "highly important to clients" for listing content: photos 73%, videos 48%.
- Sellers' agents rating "more or much more important": photos 88%, videos 47%.

Zillow Group Consumer Housing Trends Report for Agents 2025 (PDF, Oct 2025). Buyers: about 10,200 successful buyers, fielded April to September 2025. Sellers: more than 7,400 unique sellers, April to August 2025.
https://www.zillowstatic.com/bedrock/app/uploads/sites/60/2025/10/Consumer-Housing-Trends-Report-for-Agents-2025_102925.pdf
- Buyers, how they first found their agent (2025): real estate website or app 22%, friend or family referral 20%, social networking site or app 5%. Net online 33% (23% in 2018). Net referral 30%.
- Sellers who used an agent, how they first found them (2025): real estate website or app 20%, search engine 7%, social networking site or app 6%. Net online 35% (15% in 2018).
- 94% of buyers used at least one online shopping resource (website, mobile, app).
- 30% of buyers say their agent gave them a video tour (38% would ideally have one).
- 57% of sellers say their listing included a virtual tour; 66% rate a virtual tour very or extremely important; 75% are more likely to hire an agent who includes virtual tours or interactive floor plans; 78% are more likely to hire an agent who includes high-resolution photography.

Pew Research Center, "Americans' Social Media Use 2025" (20 Nov 2025): https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/11/20/americans-social-media-use-2025/
- 5,022 US adults surveyed 5 Feb to 18 June 2025: YouTube 84%, Facebook 71%, Instagram half (50%, up from 40% in 2021), TikTok 37% (up from 21% in 2021).
- Instagram: 80% of adults 18 to 29 vs 19% of adults 65 and older; women 55% vs men 44%.
- 5,123 US adults surveyed 24 Feb to 2 March 2025: 24% use TikTok daily; roughly half of 18 to 29 year-olds use TikTok daily vs 5% of those 65 and older.

Realtor.com survey release (9 Oct 2025): https://mediaroom.realtor.com/2025-10-09-82-of-Americans-Use-AI-for-Housing-Market-Information,-Realtor-com-R-Survey-Finds
- 1,000 US adults interested in buying or selling a primary home, or who did in the last two years, surveyed 7 and 8 August 2025.
- Nearly 90% rely on social media for housing information; YouTube 73% and Facebook 57% are the most used; 76% of Gen Z say TikTok is a go-to for housing content.
- "Positive use of time" for housing information: real estate agents 65.6%, social media 52.6%.
- 30.5% say social media information gave an inaccurate picture of the market.

## Our angle

- Group by the decision a realtor makes: be there? which app? does it bring leads? what do buyers and sellers want to see? what does AI change?
- Key tension to explain: 39% of agents say social gives them the most quality leads, but only 5% of buyers and 6% of sellers say they first found their agent there. Reading (advice, not a stat): social works as proof before the call, so build posts that make a stranger trust you.
- Next5 advice section (advice, not stats): 3 post types that fit the data (listing video, local tip, buyer or seller tip), slideshow format, "approve every post".
