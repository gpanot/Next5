4. **birdeye.com / hootsuite / zillow agent posts roundups** (from search snippets) — all generic idea-dump lists ("share your listing", "use video"), no fill-in-the-blank caption, no shot order, no rule about MLS timing.

**Gap to fill:** a fill-in-the-blank just-listed caption (price, beds/baths, neighborhood, one feature, CTA), a specific photo/room order, a "post the day the listing goes active" timing rule tied to MLS syndication (not a vague "a few times a week"), the same Fair Housing wording caution the open-house article already sources, and what to do with the post once the home is under contract.

## Facts and sources

1. **Clear Cooperation / MLS timing**: Reused from the already-sourced `coming-soon-real-estate-post.ts` facts (same primary sources, re-cited here since this article makes its own claim about "just listed" timing): once a seller signs and a home is filed in the MLS, syndication to the major portals (Zillow, Realtor.com) typically happens within a day or so of the MLS going active, which is why a just-listed post should go out the same day the status flips to active, not days later.
   - Source: [NAR: MLS Clear Cooperation Policy (policy text)](https://www.nar.realtor/about-nar/policies/mls-clear-cooperation-policy)
   - Source: [ARMLS: Clear Cooperation policy and what counts as public marketing](https://armls.com/nar-clear-cooperation)
2. **Fair Housing Act wording rule (reused from `open-house-social-media-post-ideas.ts`, same primary source, applies identically to a just-listed caption since it is also an ad for the sale of a dwelling)**: 42 U.S.C. 3604(c) makes it unlawful to publish any notice, statement or advertisement for the sale of a dwelling that indicates a preference, limitation or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, handicap, familial status or national origin.
   - Source: [HUD: Fair Housing Act, Section 3604(c) text via HUD Office of Fair Housing](https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/fair_housing_equal_opp/fair_housing_act_overview)
3. **Buyer/seller demand for photos and video in a new listing post** (reused, already sourced in `realEstateSocialMediaStatistics.ts`, cited again here to support the advice to lead with a strong photo/video, not text):
   - 78% of sellers are more likely to hire an agent who includes high-resolution photography; 75% more likely to hire one who includes virtual tours or interactive floor plans; 66% of sellers say a virtual tour is very/extremely important. Source: [Zillow Consumer Housing Trends Report for Agents 2025](https://www.zillowstatic.com/bedrock/app/uploads/sites/60/2025/10/Consumer-Housing-Trends-Report-for-Agents-2025_102925.pdf), October 2025.
   - 73% of buyers' agents said photos are highly important to their clients; 88% of sellers' agents said photos matter more or much more. Source: [NAR 2025 Profile of Home Staging](https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/nar-report-reveals-home-staging-boosts-sale-prices-and-reduces-time-on-market), May 2025.
