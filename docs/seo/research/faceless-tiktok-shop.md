## Facts to state, with sources

All sources fetched 2026-10-29. Primary source = TikTok Shop Academy (seller-us.tiktok.com) or
TikTok Shop policy pages.

### R1. Video guidance asks for the face and the physical product

Page: Requirements for High-Quality Videos and LIVEs.
https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4581457528243969&lang=en
VIDEO section, verbatim:
- "Create content that is recorded in a real-world environment, add some camera movement, and
  feature people to make it interesting."
- "Include engaging and dynamic visuals, ensuring they feature your face and the physical product."
- "Aim for at least 3 seconds of dynamic content without any still images or looping visuals"
- Prohibited: "Do not use still, looping, or scrolling images through the entire video without
  featuring your face and the physical product."

Note the condition in the prohibited line: it is about a whole video of still, looping or scrolling
images with no face and no physical product. A video with real movement of the product is not that.

### R2. The LIVE rules are separate and stricter

Same page, LIVE section, verbatim:
- "Interact with viewers through real-time verbal or sign language communication."
- "Offer product information with detailed explanations and live demonstrations. Your content should
  be dynamic and interactive."
- Prohibited: "Do not use non-real-time verbal interaction such as AI-generated voices, audio
  recordings, or radio."
- Prohibited: "Do not use stills, slideshows, or scrolling images that covers more than 50% of the
  screen."
- Prohibited: "Do not use animated figures or content that covers more than 50% of the screen."
- Prohibited: "Do not overlay product display page screenshots on your content."

The AI-voice ban is in the LIVE section. The video section on this page does not mention AI voices.
Do not claim AI voiceover is banned on videos.

### R3. Enforcement on that page

Same page: violations "may be subject to enforcement actions that impact your account health", and
the listed actions are removing content, removing access to features, restricting commission
earnings, and restricting or removing the creator account.

### R4. Shoppable carousels are an official format, with their own guidance

Page: How to create Shoppable Carousels.
https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6866148174104334&lang=en
Verbatim:
- Images: "At least 2", recommends "5 to 7 clear, consistent photos".
- "Use a 9:16 aspect ratio. It fills the screen and matches how shoppers browse."
- "Let the product fill roughly 40 to 60 percent of the frame"
- "Order your photos like a short story so shoppers keep swiping"
- "Add text to reinforce benefits" (1 to 3 selling points recommended)
- In the app: start a new post, add photos in order, then "Tag products. You will need to tap
  'Products' multiple times in order to add multiple product links in a single post", write the
  description, add music, publish.
- In Seller Center: Content Hub, then "Click Post on TikTok > Photo Post", link products (multiple
  products, single product or shop), review and publish.
- Avoid copyrighted images, false advertising and excessive text overlays.

This page states no face requirement. It is the one shoppable format where TikTok's own guidance
never asks for a face.

### R5. Linking a product to a video after posting

Page: How to Link Products to Videos After You've Posted.
https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5178002307598122&lang=en
Verbatim, a video can be linked only if it is: "posted in the last 30 days"; "contains sound or
music from the Commercial Music Library only"; "contains a product that matches with a corresponding
product link"; and "complies with the TikTok Shop Content Policy".
Also verbatim: creators cannot "edit or update existing product links to replace linked products in
a published video" - links can only be added to videos that do not already have them.

### R6. Seller tools that matter to a faceless seller

Page: Shoppable Video Tools to Create High-Quality Videos.
https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4123214779467563&lang=en
- Video Pre-Check: identifies "critical violation risks before publishing". Limited to 5 to 10
  checks a day depending on shop performance.
- Video Diagnosis: flags content with violations (red tags) or improvement opportunities (yellow
  tags).
- Link Products: adds product links to videos posted within the past 30 days.
- LIVE Highlights auto-posting: generates up to 5 shoppable videos per livestream.
- Direct upload from Seller Center.
None of these tools builds a video out of product images.

### R7. Creator and affiliate eligibility

Page: What You Need to Know Before Becoming a TikTok Shop Creator.
https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7608640301074219&lang=en
Verbatim: "You must be at least 18 years old." "You must be based in the United States." Affiliate
creators need "at least 1,000 followers". Creators promoting from the product marketplace need at
least 5,000 followers. Official shop creators have "No minimum follower requirement".
The page does not require appearing on camera. Identity verification does ask for a real-time ID and
selfie photo and "3 real-time selfies with your head turned" - that is verification, not content.

### R8. Affiliate Creator Pilot Program limits

Page: Affiliate Creator Pilot Program Introduction.
https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5526872163927850&lang=en
Verbatim: the program is for "Promising creators, who have yet to meet traditional requirements
(e.g. having a minimum of 5,000 followers)". Participants stay in the program at least 30 calendar
days before they can graduate; the graduation threshold is 5,000 followers. Standard pilot
participants are limited to "3 shoppable videos per day" and "3 LIVE selling streams per week".
Extended Pilot Creators (policy violations or low-quality content patterns) get 1 shoppable LIVE
weekly and 3 shoppable videos weekly.

### R9. Product image requirements

Page: Product Detail Pages & Listing Quality Guidelines.
https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=481891871868714&lang=en
Verbatim: "Your main PDP image should have a resolution > 600x600 pixels." "The first image in your
PDP should use a high-quality background. We recommend using a white background." The main image
"must not contain any text, logos, watermarks, borders, graphics covering the product, or any
staging with multiple product variants." "The first or main image in your PDP shouldn't have too
much text." The "Good" tier needs "five or more images of the main product", max 9 images, no
digital renderings, no duplication of the main image, no promotional stickers.

### NOT VERIFIED - do not state

- "Creators and merchants may post up to 30 shoppable short videos per day and up to 60 shoppable
  photo posts per day", effective 2026-05-11. Search attributed this to a seller-us.tiktok.com
  Policy Pulse page (knowledge_id=7096334743553838); both fetch attempts returned HTTP 404, so no
  primary source was read. Left out of the draft. Only the pilot-program limits in R8 are used.
- Any number of products taggable per carousel or per video (10, 20). Not in the official carousel
  page, which only lists "multiple products, single product, shop" as options.
- Any claim about faceless accounts earning more or less than accounts that show a face.
- Any claim about how the algorithm treats faceless content.
