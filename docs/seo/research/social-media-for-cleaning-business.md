# Research: social media for cleaning business

Target keyword: "social media for cleaning business" (Keyword Planner: 10/mo US avg, 40 peak month, LOW competition, industry backlog line). Hub: `industry`. Cold start, no Search Console gap fits this intent.

## Top results and what they miss

1. **ZenMaid** (https://www.zenmaid.com/magazine/social-media-marketing-for-cleaning-businesses/) — platform table (Facebook for residential/referrals, Instagram for before/after, TikTok for owner-led reach, LinkedIn for commercial contracts), posting cadence (3 posts/week + stories, batch weekly), paid ad budget suggestion ($5-10/day, 10-mile radius). Misses: no caption examples, no mention of client consent before filming inside someone's home, no specific hook lines.
2. **SocialMon** (https://www.socialmon.ai/blog/60-cleaning-business-instagram-post-ideas-with-examples) — 60 post ideas across 7 buckets (before/after, behind-the-scenes, reels, educational, promotions, social proof, fun/engagement). Pure idea dump, no sources, no posting plan, no privacy guidance, no local-trade tailoring beyond generic cleaning.
3. Other results scanned via search snippets (Picmim, Socialrails, Socialync, Spotless, JaniJobs) repeat the same before/after + CleanTok framing with no new angle: before/after is "the backbone," post 3-4x/week on IG/TikTok, point every post to a quote request.

**Gap across all of them:** none address that a cleaning business films inside other people's private homes, so every before/after post risks showing a client's address, mail, family photos, medications, or kids' rooms. None give a consent script or a checklist for what to blur/avoid before posting. This is the real differentiator for this article: practical content ideas PLUS the client-privacy step every other list skips.
