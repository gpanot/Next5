## Top 5 results for "social media for hair salons" and what they miss

1. **Hookle** – "10 Powerful Social Media Tips For Hair Salon" (https://www.hookle.net/post/10-powerful-social-media-tips-for-hair-salon) — generic tips (post before/afters, educate, show team), no cadence numbers, no booking-conversion guidance, is a soft pitch for Hookle's tool.
2. **Zolmi** – "Top 45 Hair Salon Social Media Ideas For 2025" (https://zolmi.com/salon-social-media/ideas) — long idea list (before/after, team bios, quotes, UGC reposts, giveaways). No platform-specific cadence beyond "a post a week" for one idea. Zero stats or sources. No mention of client consent, no booking-link guidance, no tagging-stylists guidance.
3. **Glammatic** – "35 Salon Social Media Content Ideas" (https://www.glammatic.com/blog/35-social-media-content-ideas-salons/) — gives cadence numbers (IG 2-7/week, FB 1-2/day, TikTok 1-4/day) but no source for them, and organizes ideas as a themed-day calendar (Makeover Monday, etc.) that reads the same as every other salon content calendar. No client consent, no booking conversion, no stylist-tagging guidance, no multi-stylist considerations. Mainly a funnel to their own management service.
4. **Appointible / Booksy / sabrinasadminservices** (various "20-100+ post ideas" roundups) — same idea-list format repeated with different numbers; none address turning a post into a booking, none address the multi-stylist / commission-chair reality of most salons, none mention asking for client consent before posting their photo.
5. **Salon-software vendor blogs** (SalonBiz, Meevo, Kitomba, Salonist, Strategies Coaching) — similar "dos and don'ts" or themed-day lists; general consistency advice ("post 2-3x/week, every week, even in your slow weeks") with no number backed by a source, same gap on consent, stylist tagging, and booking links.

**What none of them cover well, and what this article adds:**
- Concrete weekly plan with a share-of-posts breakdown (like the gym article) instead of an undifferentiated idea list.
- Multi-stylist salons: whether to post from the salon account, the stylist's own account, or both, and how to tag the stylist who did the work so their own following sees it too (matters because many stylists work on commission/booth rent and build their own clientele).
- Client consent: ask before every before/after or face post, every time, and keep track of who said yes — this is widely stated as best practice across sources but never turned into a simple routine step in any top-5 result.
- Turning views into bookings: a booking link in bio, one clear ask per post ("DM to book" / "link in bio"), replying to comments same day. Only vendor blogs gesture at this, never as a dedicated section.
