## Top 5 results and what they miss

Searched "tiktok slideshow maker" and "tiktok slideshow maker app best" (2026-11-01). The results
split into two groups, and neither gives an honest answer:

1. **Single-purpose AI tool landing pages** (ppt.ai, wavegen.ai, slidetik.ai, slidereels.com,
   slidestorm.ai, usefastlane.ai, reelbase.io). Each is a sales page for one tool, written as if it
   is the only option. None mention that TikTok's own in-app Photo Mode already does this for free,
   none compare their free-tier limits against each other, and most do not mention that you still
   upload the result to TikTok by hand.
2. **General "best slideshow app" listicles** (capcut.com/resource, presenti.ai, flexclip.com,
   sendible.com, techbloat.com). These cover general photo/video slideshow software (wedding
   videos, YouTube) and only mention TikTok as one export option among many, with no owner-focused
   take on cost or watermarks.

Gap this article fills: a plain, owner-focused answer to "do I even need a special app", a 3-way
comparison (TikTok's own tool, general design tools, single-purpose AI slideshow tools) with real
cost and watermark facts checked today, and an honest note that several of the newer tools only
differ from each other in price, not in what you get.
