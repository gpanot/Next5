  example with an exact hook line.

## Gap-fill plan for the draft

- Give a repeatable Reel structure: hook in first 1-2 seconds, 3 short shots, one on-screen line of
  text, caption with CTA. This answers "what makes a good Reel hook" from PAA.
- Give 8 to 10 reel ideas tied to real local trades (not abstract "behind the scenes" bullets):
  salon, gym, cleaner, HVAC, auto repair, dentist, restaurant, cleaning. Each with one exact line to
  say in the first second.
- State the 3-minute discovery rule and recommend 15 to 30 seconds for a first Reel, with the
  sourced reasoning.
- Section on posting with few or no followers, tying to the discovery mechanic (Explore/Reels tab
  shows Reels to non-followers regardless of follower count, which is the mechanic that makes Reels
  different from photo posts for a page starting at zero).
- Short "How Next5 does this" section: Next5 turns website photos into a Reels-ready video with a
  hook and caption already written.
