**What all of them miss**, and what this article does instead:

1. **How to actually shoot the plate on a phone, between tickets.** Every top result says "post great food photos" and none says where to stand, which angle per dish, or what to switch off.
2. **Google Business Profile as part of the same 10 minutes.** For a restaurant, "restaurants near me" is the real feed. No top result ties the photo you just took to the profile that gets found first.
3. **Which music a restaurant is actually allowed to use.** TikTok restricts Business Accounts to the Commercial Music Library, and Instagram's licensed library is not open to every account type. No top result mentions either, and it is the first thing that mutes a restaurant's post.
4. **A post whose job is to fill tonight's empty tables**, not to build a brand.

### Deliberately left out

- "Roughly three-quarters of restaurant traffic is off-premises", attributed to the National Restaurant Association by Beyond Menu. Not verified against restaurant.org this run, so it is not in the draft.
- "Instagram's per-follower engagement rate is 2.2% versus Facebook's 0.22%" and the "about 14,000 tracks" figure for Meta's Sound Collection. Both appear only in secondary blogs. Left out.
- Any claim that photos increase orders or rankings by some amount. Beyond Menu asserts a link without a number; the draft does not state one.
