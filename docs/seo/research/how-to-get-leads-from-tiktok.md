## Facts, each with source
1. A verified TikTok Business Account can set up a free "Get Leads" form in TikTok Business Suite; after TikTok approves it, a sign-up button appears on the profile and a "Get Leads" link can be added to videos and LIVEs. Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/generate-small-business-leads-on-tiktok (fetched 2026-11-11).
2. TikTok's small-business lead guidance (its own blog) centers only on paid ad campaigns (Lead Generation objective in Ads Manager, instant forms, website forms); it has no organic/free lead tactic. Source: same URL above, direct fetch 2026-11-11.
3. Bio link access: a Business account (standard or verified) can add a clickable bio link without a 1,000-follower minimum; a personal account needs 1,000 followers to unlock a clickable bio link. Consistent across multiple independent sources checked 2026-11-11: Shopify (https://www.shopify.com/blog/how-to-add-link-to-tiktok-bio), Outfy, TLinky, linke.io. Used as a checked platform fact (not health/legal/money, multi-source agreement).
4. DMs: a creator/business can allow DMs from "everyone" in privacy settings so new leads can message without being followed first; this is a standard TikTok privacy setting, not from any one secondary source, do not cite a number, keep as plain advice ("open your DMs to everyone in Settings > Privacy").
5. TikTok bios allow up to 80 characters. Source: onlysocial.io / corroborated across multiple link-in-bio guides fetched 2026-11-11 (used only as a soft platform fact for bio-writing advice, kept generic, not cited with a hard number in the article to avoid relying on a single non-primary source for a precise character count — will phrase as "a short bio" instead of citing 80 exactly).

## Decision on precision
- Will NOT cite the exact "80 characters" figure as a hard number since it is a secondary-source detail, not confirmed on TikTok's own help pages during this research session (fetch to support.tiktok.com returned empty content). Will give the advice without the number.
- Will cite the Business account bio-link rule since 3+ independent sources agree and it materially changes what the reader should do (switch to Business account early).
- Will cite TikTok's own ads-blog confirming the "Get Leads" free form in Business Suite as a legitimate free tool worth mentioning, and will note that TikTok's own guidance otherwise pushes paid ads, which is the competitive gap.
