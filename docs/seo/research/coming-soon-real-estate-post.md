| Public marketing "includes, but is not limited to" flyers in windows, yard signs, digital marketing on public facing websites (NAR's own policy text) | NAR MLS Clear Cooperation Policy page: https://www.nar.realtor/about-nar/policies/mls-clear-cooperation-policy (checked by the reviewer, 2026-10-20) |

Inference, stated as advice, not fact: a public Instagram or TikTok post is "digital marketing on a
public facing website", so treat it as public marketing unless your MLS says otherwise.
