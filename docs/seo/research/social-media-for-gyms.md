## Top 5 results and what they miss

1. **gymdesk.com/blog/gym-post-ideas** ("100 Gym Post Ideas") — closest competitor: written for
   small independent gym owners, has a TikTok section, hooks and caption advice, FAQ. Misses: no
   posting cadence tied to a weekly plan, no direct conversion step (trial offer, DM/comment CTA),
   100 ideas with no prioritization so an owner does not know where to start.
2. **wod.guru/blog/gym-social-media-post-ideas** (50+ ideas) — covers 10 platforms, shows real
   brand examples (Planet Fitness, Gold's Gym, Equinox). Misses: those examples are franchises/big
   chains, not a one-location independent gym's budget or team size; no engagement numbers; no
   single clear weekly plan.
3. **socialpilot.co/blog/social-media-for-gyms** — broad "complete strategy" piece, platform by
   platform (Instagram 4.21% engagement rate for fitness vs 1.22% cross-industry average cited).
   Misses: strategy-level advice (scheduling tools, analytics) over concrete posts an owner can
   use today; no narrow focus on "what to post" question.
4. **gymmaster.com/blog/viral-social-media-post-ideas-for-gyms** (101 ideas) — long idea list.
   Misses: no weekly cadence, no conversion step, no FAQ depth on posting frequency specifics.
5. **wellyx.com/blog/gym-social-media-post-ideas** (130 ideas, 2026) — same pattern: big list,
   little structure on what to post **this week** or how it turns into a trial sign-up.

Shared gap across all 5: none gives a simple, narrow "post these 3-5 things a week, in this order,
with this CTA" plan sized for a one-location gym with no marketing team. All are idea dumps, not
answers to the one question an owner actually has.

## People Also Ask / related questions used for FAQ

- How often should a gym post on social media? (web search, consensus across gymmaster, wodify,
  sociallyin, wellyx: 3-5x/week on Instagram/Facebook, more often — up to daily — on TikTok;
  quality over quantity)
- What is the best social media platform for a gym to get new members? (web search: Instagram
  primary discovery/social-proof platform before a trial booking; Facebook for 35+ demographic and
  local ads; TikTok best organic reach to non-followers, especially under-35, needs 3+ videos/week
  for the algorithm to reward consistency)
- Fitness content fact: TikTok fitness content has accumulated over 300 billion views on the
  platform (cited by multiple sources, e.g. andrewwallis.me / platform-reporting roundups — treated
  as a general platform-popularity note only, not a specific claim requiring a regulator-level
  source since it is not health/legal/money).
- Instagram fitness brands average 4.21% engagement rate vs 1.22% cross-industry average (cited by
  socialpilot.co/blog/social-media-for-gyms) — used only as a comparative note on which platform to
  prioritize, not as a Next5-specific or guaranteed result.
- Can you post gym content without showing your face? Not directly in PAA but a recurring reader
  question across Next5's existing faceless-content article; relevant since many solo gym
  owners/trainers are camera-shy. Answered generically (equipment/space/member shots, no invented
  stats).

## Platform facts to verify before stating
