## Top results found and what they miss

Searched: "tiktok for plumbers what to post", "plumber tiktok marketing ideas 2026", "does tiktok
work for plumbers".

- upcover.com, metricool.com, widefoc.us, plumbingwebmasters.com, frontrangemomentum.com,
  ritnerdigital.com, socialbee.com, housecallpro.com, mammothforplumbers.com: agency-style guides.
  List content ideas (before/after, how-to, behind-the-scenes) but read as ads for hiring the
  agency. None give a direct up-front answer to "does this work" or "what do I film."
- webmatter.co.uk ("TikTok for Plumbers: Does It Actually Work?") is the most honest of the
  bunch: says TikTok is "not a silver bullet for lead generation," a brand/trust-building
  platform, results are gradual. No concrete posting plan.
- elev8operations.com ("Contractor TikTok Marketing 2026: The Honest Guide") is the closest
  match to the angle we want: explicit that "homeowners rarely hire straight from TikTok,"
  recommends tracking how many new customers mention it, gives effort-scaled posting frequency
  (3-5 posts/week to start), and names a working content idea: drain camera footage, recurring
  "worst install I've fixed" series.
- None of the above address what NOT to film (a customer's home, bathroom, personal info) or the
  trust/privacy angle specific to being a stranger inside someone's house, which is a real gap for
  a trade that works inside private homes.
- None give a short, direct answer box. Most bury the "does it work" answer under 1,500+ words of
  generic social media advice reused across every trade.

## Facts and sources

1. **37% of US adults use TikTok** (63% of under-30s, smaller shares of older adults), from a
   survey of 5,022 US adults conducted Feb 5 - Jun 18, 2025, up from 21% in 2021 (fastest-growing
   platform Pew tracks).
   Source: [Pew Research Center, "Americans' Social Media Use 2025" (Nov 20, 2025)](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/11/20/americans-social-media-use-2025/)
2. **TikTok rarely produces a direct booked job for home service trades**; the real payoff is
   brand familiarity that helps paid ads convert and word-of-mouth, so track how many new
   customers mention seeing you on TikTok rather than expecting instant calls. Advice/practitioner
   observation, not a hard stat.
   Source: [Elev8 Operations, "Contractor TikTok Marketing 2026: The Honest Guide"](https://www.elev8operations.com/guides/contractor-tiktok-marketing-2026)
3. **TikTok is a brand/trust-building platform for trades, not a lead-generation silver bullet**,
   and results are gradual rather than immediate. Advice/practitioner observation.
   Source: [Web Matter, "TikTok for Plumbers: Does It Actually Work?"](https://webmatter.co.uk/tiktok-for-plumbers-does-it-actually-work/)
4. Content ideas used across sources (advice, not stats): quick-fix/DIY tips, before-and-after
   clearouts and pipe replacements, day-in-the-life, drain camera footage, product/tool demos,
   myth-busting, duet-style reactions to other people's plumbing mistakes, winterizing outdoor
   faucets.
   Sources: [Upcover, "How to Use TikTok as a Plumber to Promote Your Business"](https://www.upcover.com/blog/how-to-use-tiktok-as-a-plumber),
   [Elev8 Operations](https://www.elev8operations.com/guides/contractor-tiktok-marketing-2026)
5. Posting frequency: 3 to 5 posts a week to start is the common recommendation for a business new
   to TikTok, scaling up with more effort. Advice.
   Source: [Elev8 Operations, "Contractor TikTok Marketing 2026: The Honest Guide"](https://www.elev8operations.com/guides/contractor-tiktok-marketing-2026)

Not used: the "341.2 million views, top 3 most-viewed trade" stat that appears in some search
summaries has no traceable primary source across the pages checked (housecallpro.com cites no
such number). Left out per "when in doubt, leave it out."
