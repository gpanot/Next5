## Top 5 results and what they miss

1. Descript (descript.com/blog/article/how-to-make-slideshow-on-tiktok): has 6 steps and photo specs
   (9:16, 1080x1920, min 1080x1080, JPEG/PNG), but its own FAQ coverage misses audio copyright
   guidance, optimal duration per photo, and the swipeable vs auto-play distinction is only mentioned
   in passing, not explained with an action (which button gives which result).
2. Shopify PK (shopify.com/pk/blog/how-to-make-a-slideshow-on-tiktok): explains auto-play (Templates)
   vs swipeable (Photo Mode) and recommends min 1080x1920 for full-screen, warns unlicensed music can
   get a post muted, taken down, or hit with a DMCA notice. Misses: what to do if Photo Mode is not
   visible on an account, and does not say any of this is specific to business/Commercial Music Library
   accounts.
3. SocialBee, Quso, SocialRails, Spocket, Multilogin, usefastlane (from the first search's result
   list): same shape as the two above, generic "open app, tap +, pick photos, add music, post" steps
   with templates mentioned, found via WebSearch snippet only (not fetched, no direct quotes used).
4. TikTok's own Discover page: "How to Make A TikTok Slideshow When You Dont Have Photo Mode" exists
   as a tag/page on tiktok.com, confirming this is a real, common problem (found via WebSearch result
   title only, not fetched for quotes).
5. Common gap across all: none say the autoplay-vs-swipeable choice depends on Templates vs Upload +
   Photo Mode in one clear line, and none address what a business account (which uses TikTok's
   Commercial Music Library, per the site's own existing hooks article) should do when the account
   does not show Photo Mode as an option.

## People also ask / related real questions

- What is the difference between a TikTok slideshow (photo carousel) made with Templates vs Photo
  Mode (auto-play vs swipeable)? (Source: Shopify PK fetch, WebFetch quote above)
- What if I don't see Photo Mode / the upload-photos option on my account? (Source: TikTok's own
  Discover page title, "How to Make A TikTok Slideshow When You Dont Have Photo Mode", found in
  WebSearch result list)
- Can I mix photos and video clips in one TikTok slideshow? (Source: WebSearch answer box, second
  WebSearch call, "Yes, you can mix both photos and short video clips in your TikTok slideshow")
- What size should my photos be for a TikTok slideshow? (Source: Descript and Shopify PK WebFetch:
  9:16 / 1080x1920 recommended, 1080x1080 minimum, JPG or PNG)
- Already answered in the live article's FAQ: can I make a slideshow on a computer, how many photos
  max (35), why does it post as a video, can each slide have different music (no, one sound for the
  whole post).
